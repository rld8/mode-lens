# Controlled cantilever experiment protocol

## Equipment and safety

Use a plastic ruler or thin wooden strip, a stable table, books or a small clamp, a
high-contrast background, a ruler in the same image plane and a phone capable of at
least 60 fps. Do not use glass, brittle specimens or loads that can be released toward
people. ModeLens is not suitable for buildings, bridges or safety decisions.

## Capture

1. Fix 15–20% of the specimen and measure the free length, width and thickness.
2. Lock exposure/focus when the phone permits it. Mount the phone perpendicular to the
   motion plane and keep the reference scale in that plane.
3. Record 5–10 seconds. Deflect the free end gently, release it without pushing sideways
   and do not touch the camera or table.
4. Record three repetitions without changing the framing.
5. For a controlled modification, add a light clip near the free end and repeat all
   three captures.

## Acceptance log

Record the phone, nominal FPS, lighting, distance, dimensions, scale uncertainty,
material assumption and any change between repetitions. Reject a take when the support
moves visibly, the beam leaves the ROI, the image clips, or the target mode approaches
the conservative `0.4 × FPS` band limit.

## Interpretation

Report the three first-mode frequencies, their coefficient of variation and the exact
configuration. A target below 5% variation is an experiment goal, not a guaranteed
result. If intervals overlap or tracking is poor, report the comparison as inconclusive.
