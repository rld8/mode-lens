## Synthetic controlled comparison

The baseline modes were 3.499963 Hz and 21.910112 Hz. The modified ground truth used
3.10 Hz and 18.60 Hz; ModeLens measured 3.1001 Hz and 18.5970 Hz.

| Pair | MAC | Δf/f | Label | Reason |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| 1 → 1 | 0.999975 | -11.426% | `measurable_change` | Frequency intervals did not overlap. |
| 2 → 2 | 0.850508 | -15.121% | `inconclusive` | Point estimates differed, but broad intervals overlapped. |

The second result demonstrates the intended conservative behaviour: a large point
difference does not become a confident claim when uncertainty is too broad.
